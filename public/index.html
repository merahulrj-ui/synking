Frequently Asked Questions

Is Synkin free to use?

+
Yes! Synkin is free to download and explore. You can discover nearby vibes, chat, and connect in real-time with verified members.

What is the 18+ requirement?

+
Synkin is strictly designed for adults aged 18 and above. We enforce age verification and actively remove underage accounts to maintain a safe, mature dating ecosystem.

Can someone take screenshots of my photos or calls?

+
No. Synkin implements Android FLAG_SECURE DRM protection. Screen captures and screen recordings are automatically blocked at the hardware level.

How does Picture-in-Picture (PiP) work?

+
During any active video call, pressing the Home button or tapping the Chat button seamlessly activates the native Android OS Picture-in-Picture window. You can browse other apps or text your match while your video call continues smoothly.
